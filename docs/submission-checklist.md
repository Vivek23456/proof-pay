# Submission checklist — Frontier 2026

All items below require the human builder (me, the agent, cannot impersonate the submitter on arena.colosseum.org). This is the May 10–11 runbook.

## May 10 — submission day

- [ ] Open [arena.colosseum.org](https://arena.colosseum.org) and log in with the Colosseum account linked to this build.
- [ ] Find the Frontier 2026 submission form.
- [ ] Confirm the "no traditional tracks" wording matches [docs/prize-lane.md](./prize-lane.md). If tracks are back, re-read the submission page end-to-end and pick Grand Champion primary.
- [ ] Paste the **2-sentence pitch** from [docs/competitive-landscape.md](./competitive-landscape.md):
      > ProofPay is what Decal can't be: your Solana purchase history is yours, not each merchant's database. Pay once, every future merchant who opts in can trust you — and price accordingly.
- [ ] Attach the demo video URL (YouTube unlisted + Loom backup).
- [ ] Attach the GitHub URL. Make sure the default branch is `main` and the README is current.
- [ ] Attach the live Vercel URL.
- [ ] If **University Award** eligible (verify current Frontier rules on the Arena page): tick the checkbox, add institution. Otherwise leave untouched.
- [ ] Do **not** re-open `docs/mvp-spec.md` to add features. Anything that isn't in the demo video is a v2 item per [docs/v2.md](./v2.md).
- [ ] Submit.
- [ ] Take a screenshot of the "submission received" screen. Keep it in case a judge pings.

## May 11 — buffer day

**Only** use this day for:
- Responding to a Colosseum judge question on Discord / email.
- Fixing a broken quickstart if a tester surfaces one.
- Re-uploading the video if it got silently de-ranked by YouTube.

**Do not** use this day for:
- Adding new features.
- Rewriting the pitch copy.
- Switching the primary prize lane (that decision is locked at Phase 0).
- Deploying to mainnet.

## If submission goes late

- [ ] Email hello@colosseum.com *before* the deadline if there's a blocker.
- [ ] Post in the Colosseum Discord `#help` channel with specifics.
- [ ] Accept the outcome. A good submission on May 11 beats a great submission on May 12.

## Post-submission (May 12+)

- [ ] Move any new ideas into [docs/v2.md](./v2.md).
- [ ] Close `docs/mvp-spec.md`. Archive as `docs/archive/mvp-spec-v1.md` before starting v2.
- [ ] Share the demo video on X / Solana Discord / relevant subreddits.
- [ ] Reach out to 2 Decal / Humanship / Squads builders for a "what you wish ProofPay supported" conversation.
- [ ] Rest. The accelerator decision timeline from Colosseum is ~3 weeks.
